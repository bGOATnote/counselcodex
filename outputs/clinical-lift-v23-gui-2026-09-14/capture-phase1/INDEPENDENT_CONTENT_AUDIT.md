# Independent content audit of the eight phase-1 GUI attempts

Scope: read-only review of the retained patient messages, first producer drafts, final published answers, issued events, cited passages and reviewer/repair outputs in this directory. Content was inspected before reading the reviewer findings. No new model calls, clinical actions, source refresh, reference-label changes or runtime edits were performed. This is an engineering audit of clinical text, not independent physician adjudication or a clinical-accuracy score.

## What the eight completions establish

- Eight attempts completed across **seven original cases**: C13 was assessed both before and after an added gradual-onset statement. Four attempts used a patch and a fresh review; four did not.
- **None of the eight first Opus dispositions changed during repair.** The useful repairs addressed patient instructions, fact scope, source applicability and differential wording. Separately, two *issued early actions* were reconciled: C16 ED-now to a supported same-day alternative, and C48 same-day to emergency/EMS.
- All eight final reviews accepted. That does not demonstrate eight clinically correct trajectories: C48 still had an inadequate early instruction, and narrower final-text/source issues remain below.
- Final completion ranged from 41.292 to 79.613 seconds, median 58.707 seconds. These are server/run times, not paint measurements or a controlled latency comparison. Two nonblocking questions were useful conversation events, not completed dispositions. C48's nonblocking fall-mechanism question also did not provide the missing assisted-transport instruction.

## Source scope and the useful next test

The retained evidence is traceable, but most cited material in these attempts is consumer education or agent-compiled emergency-department synthesis, not a primary telehealth routing protocol. For example, C06's [NHLBI blood-pressure categories](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/high-blood-pressure/diagnosis) and [MedlinePlus mechanism explanation](https://medlineplus.gov/bloodpressuremedicines.html) do not approve a refill. C30's [MedlinePlus poison-ivy summary](https://medlineplus.gov/poisonivyoakandsumac.html) supports pharmacist-guided OTC advice; the delayed-spread explanation is attributed separately to the retained OpenEM synthesis. The final replies mostly acknowledge this distinction. A reachable link and exact quote still do not prove applicability.

Do not turn all residual imprecision into additional serial model repair. First correct the known early mobility/transport failure, then test a small set of explicit boundaries: current control versus longitudinal stability; reported rash distribution versus absence elsewhere; preserved drinking versus assessed hydration; and recommendation for same-day access versus confirmed availability. Evaluate changes in issued patient instructions as well as final text. A successful criterion is fewer unsupported facts or unnecessary/delayed care actions without new emergency misses—not more accept verdicts.

Historical captures, first drafts, criticism and final answers remain unchanged. This audit does not report pending v23 full-cohort results or claim the C48 early-action defect is fixed.
