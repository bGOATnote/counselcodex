## General guidance policy

General OTC options and verified label explanations may meet a request without a clinician. Individualized treatment choices require applicable product information and relevant warnings; a response that omits the requested decision must not claim it completed that decision. If necessary individualized assessment remains, identify the task and assign Standard or Priority async based on its actual timing consequence. Neither missing history nor failed eligibility for one guideline automatically warrants escalation.

This is an engineer-authored project capability policy, **not Counsel's private protocol** or a rule that all OTC selection/dose questions legally require a physician. FDA explicitly supports consumer selection/use through Drug Facts labeling. Labels distinguish ingredients, uses, warnings and directions, and unresolved questions can be taken to a health professional. [FDA Drug Facts](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/understanding-over-counter-medicines/over-counter-drug-facts-label).

## Application contract and limits

Bind authored application constraints to actual source/version/passage hashes. State the complete qualifying source clause and its necessary patient/examination prerequisites. Unknown is not met or denied. A quoted symptom report is not a verified examination. A general or conditional statement is distinct from claiming current patient eligibility.

The initial checker is deliberately **partial**: exact source/citation identity, exact authored claim-span checks and bounded known false applications. For contracted citations the candidate also emits structured condition bindings: source condition ID, general/conditional/patient use, reported-met/reported-not-met/unknown, and original patient spans. Missing, duplicate or unresolved prerequisites cannot pass as established patient eligibility. Exact spans prove identity, not that the model interpreted their meaning correctly; a reported examination is not independently verified. It does not solve arbitrary semantic entailment, validate patient history, or certify clinical safety. Uncovered claims remain `not_assessed`. A caught error can withhold a new experimental release, but cannot manufacture an emergency, remove prior care, or silently alter the disposition. Free-form paraphrases can evade bounded checks; that is a measured limitation, not a pass.

Clinical boundaries reviewed on 2026-09-15:

- AOM observation guidance assumes diagnostic findings and a qualifying mild presentation; school attendance is not an otoscopic examination. [CDC outpatient pediatric guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/hcp/clinical-care/pediatric-outpatient.html).
- Daytime burden and the chronic insomnia duration/frequency threshold are different questions. Continuing activities does not erase reported fatigue, and the chronic definition does not establish a waiting period before assessment. [NHLBI diagnosis](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/insomnia/diagnosis).
- An Ottawa exclusion depends on the applicable examination and weight-bearing criteria, not partial weight-bearing language. A rule without its qualifying source in the supplied packet must remain unbound. [NSW adult Ottawa rules](https://aci.health.nsw.gov.au/ecat/appendices/ottawa-ankle-adult).

These sources establish conditions, not our five-route labels. Existing retrieved originals retain their own provenance; this policy document is not an answer citation and adds no source to the frozen corpus.
